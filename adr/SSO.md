# Architectural Decision: SSO Integration

## Decision
SAML preferred over OAuth for seamless corporate adoption.

## Rationale
Corporations widely adopt SAML for secure and scalable SSO integration.

## Alternatives
1. OAuth
   - Pros: Broad support across modern apps.
   - Cons: More suited for non-enterprise integrations.

## Consequences
Seamless organizational compatibility but requires robust SAML setup.
